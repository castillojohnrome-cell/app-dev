# 🎬 My All-Time Favorite Movies

Welcome to my first GitHub repository! Here is a curated list of my favorite films—stories filled with heart, inspiration, and unforgettable lessons.

---

## 🏆 Top Picks

1. **A Man Called Otto** *(2022)* – A touching reminder that unexpected friendships can bring warmth back into life.
2. **Dead Poets Society** *(1989)* – An inspiring classic about seizing the day and finding your own voice.
3. **Matilda** *(1996)* – A nostalgic favorite celebrating intelligence, kindness, and standing up for what's right.
4. **Me Before You** *(2016)* – An emotional story about living boldly and cherishing every moment.
5. **Bridge to Terabithia** *(2007)* – A heartfelt tale of imagination, friendship, and growing up.
6. **Soul** *(2020)* – A visually stunning reflection on purpose, passion, and appreciating everyday life.
